# Frames are paced at 60 a second (the rest of 16.67 ms slept between them), as a window runs, here and in frame-times-async.txt. Back to back, with no idle time, the same frames take about a fifth as long (median 0.170 ms unpaced against 0.829 ms paced, open connection, 0 ms, measured 2026-11-03 13:3x): the CPU stays busy and its caches warm. The 09:08 capture was unpaced, which is why its 0.159 ms median sat below the worker's paced 1.3 ms: the harness, not the worker.
# This naive version rebuilds the context in the frame on purpose (AppContext::rebuild, the line below). A new connection is one round trip longer than at 09:08: each session sets this machine's time zone once it opens. A query right after another on the same pool can open a second connection: sqlx pings a released connection before it's idle again.
